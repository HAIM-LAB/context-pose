\tabledesc{Each row is a ContextPose model given the ticked input streams. The reference rows are the runs of the pose-free stream ablation (\code{tab:eavf} in eclip\_streams.tex); the other rows add one or both video streams.
\emph{Video streams} (\code{asd\_contextpose.video\_features}): at each of the 32 steps of the pre-event, event and post-event windows, a 16-frame window at 15 fps (about 1 s) centred on the step is cropped to the child (a square of 4.5 shoulder widths around the tracked shoulder midpoint, interpolated across untracked frames; the full frame when a clip has no track).
\begin{itemize}
  \item VST: a frozen Kinetics-400 Video Swin Transformer (torchvision Swin3D-B, ImageNet-22K and Kinetics-400 weights); its last-stage tokens averaged over space and time (1024-d).
  \item H: hand-designed HOG-HOF descriptors after Kone\v{c}n\'{y} and Hagara (2013): gradient-orientation histograms of the window's middle frame and optical-flow-orientation histograms over the window (4$\times$4 cells), with the crop's median flow (camera motion and whole-body drift) removed, plus two log motion energies (274-d).
\end{itemize}
Each video stream enters with a child-tracked flag, gets its own temporal encoder and modality embedding, and its tokens join those the context query attends over (\code{--stream}). Stream dropout hides each stream of a training sample with probability 0.3.
\emph{Offsets and protocol:} as in \code{tab:eavf}: rows with A start from the event-annotation lookup and learn a correction, rows without A from a constant fitted on the training folds; 153 development clips, video-grouped 5-fold cross-validation with duplicate videos merged, cross-entropy against the final annotation's binary classes, identical training settings for every row.
\emph{Targets and metrics:} as in \code{tab:eavf}: classes of the final annotation, positive valence / high arousal when its score is $> 0$ (valence: 153 clips, 122 positive; arousal: 153 clips, 36 high); the ensemble's call is the majority of the 5 seeds' calls and AUC uses the mean seed score; balanced accuracy with 95\% participant-bootstrap intervals. Bold marks the best model in each column.}
