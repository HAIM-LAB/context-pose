\tabledesc{Each row is a ContextPose model given the ticked input streams, used alone, in pairs, in threes and all together; pose is never an input. EmotionCLIP enters as the subject stream in place of pose (frozen 512-d EmotionCLIP frame embeddings at each of the 32 steps of the pre-event, event and post-event windows, with a child-tracked flag).
\emph{Streams:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item E: frozen EmotionCLIP frame embeddings, attended over by the context query as pose is in the stream-ablation table (\code{tab:streams} in test.tex).
  \item A: the four binary event indicators coded by annotators.
  \item V: a 384-d MiniLM embedding of Qwen3-Omni scene descriptions.
  \item F: frozen FER ViT face embeddings, kept only on steps that pass the frontal, YuNet-detector and brightness quality gate.
\end{itemize}
\emph{Offsets:} rows with A start from the event-annotation lookup's log-odds and learn a correction to it; rows without A start from a constant offset fitted on the training folds, so no annotation information reaches them.
\emph{Protocol:} as in the stream-ablation table (\code{tab:streams} in test.tex): 153 development clips, video-grouped 5-fold cross-validation with duplicate videos merged, cross-entropy against the final annotation's binary classes, identical training settings for every row (width 64, dropout 0.3, weight decay 0.05, learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$, up to 60 epochs with patience 10). Rows without E have the settings of the stream-ablation table (\code{tab:streams} in test.tex).
\emph{Targets:} the final annotation (the confidence-weighted consensus of the three annotators): valence positive (score $> 0$) vs not positive (153 clips, 122 positive); arousal high (score $> 0$) vs low (153 clips, 36 high). Models are trained and scored on these classes.
\emph{Metrics:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Calls are the majority of the 5 seeds' calls; AUC uses the mean seed score. Decision thresholds are set on each model's own training clips, never on the scored fold.
  \item Balanced accuracy is the mean per-class recall (chance 50\%), with 95\% participant-bootstrap intervals in brackets.
  \item F1 is for the positive class (positive valence; high arousal). MCC is the Matthews correlation coefficient between calls and labels ($-1$ to 1, 0 for chance or a constant call).
\end{itemize}
\emph{References:} each annotator against the other two where those agree (a human-agreement reference; the final annotation includes each annotator's own vote, so scoring an annotator against it would be circular), always predicting the training folds' majority class, and the non-learned lookup (training-fold share of positive clips per indicator combination). Bold marks the best model in each column.}

\tabledesc{Each row is the model of the same row in Table~\ref{tab:eavf}, trained to regress the final annotation's score ($[-1, 1]$ scale) instead of classifying its sign.
\emph{Protocol:} as in the stream-ablation regression table (\code{tab:streams\_reg} in test.tex): 153 development clips, video-grouped 5-fold cross-validation with duplicate videos merged, squared error on the final annotation, identical training settings for every row; predictions are averaged over 5 seeds. Rows without E are the runs of the stream-ablation regression table (\code{tab:streams\_reg} in test.tex).
\emph{Offsets:} rows with A start from the training folds' median label per indicator combination (the lookup) and learn a correction; rows without A start from the training folds' overall median, so no annotation information reaches them.
\emph{Metrics:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Spearman is the rank correlation between prediction and label; CCC is the concordance correlation coefficient, which also penalises bias and a compressed scale (0 for a constant prediction).
  \item MAE is the mean absolute error on the $[-1, 1]$ scale.
  \item AP is the average precision with which the prediction ranks the clips with a positive final annotation (positive valence; high arousal) above the rest; mAP is its mean over valence and arousal.
  \item MCC is the Matthews correlation coefficient between the signs of the prediction and of the label ($>0$ vs $\le 0$); it is 0 when every prediction falls on one side of 0, as it does for most valence rows (80\% of the labels are positive).
\end{itemize}
\emph{References:} the training folds' overall median and the lookup are non-learned. The overall median is constant within a fold, so its Spearman and AP reflect only the differences between folds' medians. Bold marks the best model in each column.}
